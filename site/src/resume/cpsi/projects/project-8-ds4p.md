Technical lead for privacy and data-segmentation initiative enabling patients, practitioners, and organizations to identify sensitive clinical information and prevent inappropriate disclosure. Designed a standards-based architecture combining security classification and labeling with context-aware policy enforcement based on requester, recipient, purpose of use, consent, and jurisdiction. Work spans FHIR/C-CDA DS4P and related standards, with regulatory drivers including California AB 352 and similar jurisdiction-specific health privacy requirements.
